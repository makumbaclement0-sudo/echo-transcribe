How to read this

  • Consistently positive means every payment in the lookback window was above zero, and the market paid funding for the whole window. One negative print and it is out.
  • APR 5d is the sum of all payments in the window, divided by the days, times 365. It is simple interest on the notional of one leg.
  • APR 24h uses only the most recent day. Worst day uses the weakest of the five days. A 24h figure well below the 5-day figure means the carry is fading.
  • Projections hold a rate flat for the whole hold: the base case uses the 5-day average, the conservative case the worst day and the recent case the last 24h. Net subtracts taker fees to open and close both legs.
  • Break-even is how many days of funding at the 5-day rate it takes to pay those fees back.
  • Capital needed is about size × (1 + 1/perp leverage). At 2× on the short that is 1.5 × size, so yield on capital is lower than APR on notional.

Funding rates mean-revert and can flip within hours. Five clean days is a filter, not a forecast. The main risks on a 40-day hold are a basis blow-out, liquidation of the short in a squeeze, the venue cutting the funding interval, and spot/perp price divergence at exit.
